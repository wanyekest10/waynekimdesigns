A smart fan that rewards discipline with airflow. Built with Hun Han in Prototyping as a Process at CIID, taught by Jose Chavarria and Andrea Desiato.

## The brief

Design a fan that is both lightweight and serious. We took "serious" literally: a fan that holds you to your word.

## How it works

The fan watches whether you meet a goal you set. Meet it, and you get a cool breeze. Miss it, and the fan stops and a red LED comes on. It's a small feedback loop that nudges you to correct course without a lecture.

<div class="flow" role="list" aria-label="How the fan responds">
  <div class="step"><span class="n">01</span><b>Set a goal</b><small>Something small, like finishing a task.</small></div>
  <span class="to" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <div class="step focal"><span class="n">02</span><b>Meet it</b><small>The fan spins up and cools you down.</small></div>
  <span class="to" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <div class="step"><span class="n">03</span><b>Miss it</b><small>The fan pauses and the red LED lights.</small></div>
</div>
<p class="flow-note">↻ Get back on track, and the breeze comes back.</p>

We prototyped in cardboard and paper first, then wired in a motor and LED.
