A shirt for the place where my CIID classmates and I ate most of our year in Bergamo. Teşekkür ederim, and grazie di tutto.

## The shop

Tamer Kebap was cheap, fast, and right on the way home. For a cohort of grad students running on prototypes and deadlines, it was the default answer to "where should we eat?" It was also where we celebrated.

When I left in fall 2025, I missed it more than I expected. I wanted to leave the group something we could hold onto.

## The stitch

The idea showed up a year later, in New York, while I was mending a pair of shorts. I'd sewn over a CIID logo, and the stitches running through the two I's made them look like kebab spits. That was it.

## Build, test, repeat

I tried six layouts, mixing "Tamer Kebab Eating Club," "Fueled by Kebab," and build.test.repeat, the loop we lived by at CIID. The one that stuck turned "repeat" into "rep-eat," with the eat in blue.

<figure class="medium"><img src="/play/tamer-kebap-assets/concepts.webp" alt="Six shirt concepts, front and back, testing the CIID kebab mark with different slogans and colors"/><figcaption>Six concepts, front and back.</figcaption></figure>

My classmates liked it enough to ask for their own. I'm planning to give one to the crew at Tamer, and maybe bring a stack to our reunion in 2027.

## Parting thought

The best ideas here came from detours: a kebab shop on the way home, a pair of torn shorts. I hope to get back to Bergamo one day and say hello in person.
